# GUPPY program catalog

GUPPY was a suite of NSA cryptanalysis programs from the 1960s. This is a
list of its 89 programs, each with a one-line description.

**Source unverified.** The list was taken from a planning document that
cites no source. Entries marked "(Function to be determined)" suggest it
was copied from a real document, but the names and descriptions have not
been checked against one.

`aldegonde.analysis.krakup` uses the numbering below (KRAKUP is #37).

1. **ASKIT**: Predicts or evaluates results of polyalphabetic depth search based on Kappa test
2. **BALK**: Prints worksheet, 3 to 100 characters per line
3. **BAYOU**: Prints monographic and digraphic frequency counts, log and category weights for chained, disjointed or transposition digraphs
4. **BDELT**: Makes Baudot horizontal or intermessage difference streams
5. **BEE**: Prints binary 5-level differences and statistics
6. **BIGSTET**: Standard diagnostic STET tests on option, some with thresholds, but handles more data, widths, intervals and prints columnar counts
7. **BISEC**: Key recovery and decryption via generatrices and scores, for monoalphabetic in fixed-length-section cipher
8. **BREN**: Route and grille transposition decrypt, span 450
9. **BUNK**: Key drag and difference stream, Baudot arithmetic
10. **CALC**: Desk calculator functions: +,-,×,÷, exponentiation, square root, number base change
11. **CASANOVA**: Periodic polyalphabetic intermessage depth search, individual or all monographic column pairs on a width
12. **CHICKADEE**: Diagnoses and exploits stagger bust
13. **COLLEEN**: Mono-, di-, and trigraphic columnar counts and statistics on a width
14. **COPPERHEAD**: Polyalphabetic polygraphic depth search
15. **CRAZYQUILT**: Transposition bust exploitation
16. **CROSSUM**: Cross-product sums and repeat rates at all slides for all pairs of N frequency distributions
17. **DELPHI**: Key recovery and decryption, periodic polyalphabetic, related or unrelated alphabets
18. **DELT**: Horizontal or intermessage differences or sums, modular or Baudot arithmetic
19. **DELTBDELT**: (Combined function program)
20. **DIANA**: Digraphic counts and statistics
21. **DOBE**: Uniliteral (1 for 1) substitution decrypt or conversion
22. **DOBE2**: Biliteral (2 for 1) substitution decrypt or conversion
23. **DOODLE**: Formatted worksheets, specified hits underlined. Hat and crenelated diagrams
24. **DOPESHEET**: Probabilistic worksheet for polyalphabetic depth reading
25. **EPICTETUS**: Enciphered indicator search
26. **FINKSBURG**: Diagnostics on levels of 5-level streams
27. **FLUSH**: Aperiodic polyalphabetic depth search
28. **FREQWIDTH**: Prints formatted worksheet, with count below each group
29. **GEEWHIZZER**: Anagrams columnar and grille transposition
30. **GEORGE**: General purpose encipher/decipher of transposition, monoalphabetic and polyalphabetic substitution and Hagelin. Related or unrelated alphabets
31. **GIMP**: Polyalphabetic crib drag
32. **GROUPDATA**: Prints formatted worksheets
33. **HUSHPUPPY**: Polyalphabetic crib and key drag. Monographic log weights
34. **INDEX**: Index and frequency counts, user-specified sort order
35. **ISOM**: Locates isomorphs
36. **JEZEBEL**: Decrypts biliteral substitution. Coordinates may be summed, with variants, or appear nonconsecutively in cipher
37. **KRAKUP**: Tests for cyclic phenomena in nonhomogeneous material
38. **KYOTO**: Tests and exploits stagger bust situation in polyalphabetics
39. **LACER**: Interlaces 2 data streams to user specification
40. **LAMBROS**: Key recovery and decryption via generatrices and scores for periodic polyalphabetic
41. **LILINDEX**: Index and frequency counts, user-specified sort order, limited amount of data
42. **LOGDIFF**: Computes monographic plain and theoretical difference log weights
43. **MARTEE**: Recovers key length for monoalphabetic-in-fixed-length encipherment
44. **MASK**: Deletes characters or levels on cycling basis
45. **MODIRA**: Coordinate recovery for monome-dinome
46. **MONDIN**: Prints monome-dinome worksheets and decrypts
47. **MONDITRI**: Mono-, di-, and trigraphic frequency counts of selected levels and level combinations
48. **MONOSEC**: Replaces MARTEE (same options)
49. **MYSTARS**: Sorts 2-5 character groups from 1 stream; differences and sorts differences from 2 streams
50. **NEPTUNE**: Decrypts transposition within span of 100
51. **OVERLAP**: (Function to be determined)
52. **PASDEDEUX**: (Function to be determined)
53. **PICKWICK**: Theoretical cipher distribution and log weights for polyalphabetics
54. **POLLY**: Lists overall and on-cut polygraphic repeats. Statistics
55. **PROFILE**: Displays triliteral frequency distribution a la MC-I, pg. 72
56. **PUSHUP**: Tests polyalphabetic depths and prints depth reader's worksheet
57. **QUIKROB**: Polyalphabetic depth test, modified Kappa scoring on limited data
58. **QUIKSTET**: STET on limited data
59. **QUIKTWIST**: TWIST on limited data
60. **QUIKWHIZ**: GEEWHIZZER on limited data
61. **QUIKXIBAR**: XIBAR on limited data. No frequency counts option
62. **RITWIDTH**: General purpose worksheet preparation, user specifications
63. **ROBIN**: Polyalphabetic depth search
64. **ROLLFAST**: Generatrices for 1 stream or pairs of 2 or 3 streams, formatted output
65. **RUMDUM**: Sorts message identification streams prepared for INDEX
66. **SALLY**: Prints monome-dinome frequency count
67. **SCOOT**: Polyalphabetic crib and key drag. Tetragraphic weights and cribs from TAPIR
68. **SHADOW**: Profile monographic frequency count on data and on horizontal delta. Statistics
69. **SMARTSTET**: STET plus chi-square; threshold option
70. **STET**: Prints standard diagnostic statistics and counts
71. **STUBBY**: Remainder test
72. **SUMDIF**: (Function to be determined)
73. **SYLLABLE**: General purpose matrix decrypt (up to 36x36), plain and cipher unit sizes 1-5
74. **SYNDROME**: Coordinate recovery, worksheets, frequency counts and decryption for monome-dinome
75. **TABLES**: Tailor-made mathematical tables: chi-square and binomial probabilities; prime factors and numbers; combinations N things at a time; transposition column factors and matrix widths
76. **TAPECON**: Produces hard copy from paper tape, acting on functions
77. **TAPIR**: Alphabetic, inverse frequency lists and log weights for 3, 4, 5 character groups
78. **TASKAN**: Single, double transposition key test
79. **THUD**: Makes depth reader's worksheet
80. **TREES**: General purpose book-breaker's package: counts, indexes, WMP's, codebooks, et al
81. **TWIST**: Single, double transposition decrypt
82. **UNICORN**: Stripped-down version of SHADOW
83. **UNLACER**: Creates 2 data streams from 1 according to user specifications
84. **VIGORO**: Creates streams of X's and O's from 5 or 6 level tape
85. **WARP**: Difference or decrypt polyalphabetic substitution
86. **WENDY**: Prints binary worksheet (X's and O's) from 5 level characters
87. **WIDTH**: Prints frequency counts and statistics for columns of width write-out
88. **XIBAR**: Makes frequency counts overall on individual messages or on columns of width and subdivides them into homogeneous sets
89. **XPAN**: Creates data stream expanded positionally by specified characters
